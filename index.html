ARF bracket breakdown
ARF brackets (since Feb 2017): 15% on first S$5,000 · 50% on S$5,001–S$10,000 · 100% above S$10,000.
Excise duty is 12% of OMV (flat). GST is 9% of OMV (flat). Both are shown per bracket for transparency but calculated on total OMV.
COE, dealer margins, and other registration fees are excluded. Verify current rates at lta.gov.sg.
